Notes

Decisions made

  1. Placement A, recommended: a 52 pt round chip to the right of the orb, centred on it. The chip colour is paper-2, the glyph is ink, with no accent, ring or motion (DESIGN 1a). Right side, for the thumb. B puts it in the button row, left of Cancel. B is quieter, but it is 44 pt beside a text button and reads as secondary, while the founder made photos a default next to voice.
  2. Tap opens a native menu (UIMenu): Take Photo, Photo Library. A tap stops listening. If words were already heard, they go out as their own task, as Done would.
  3. Permission on first Take Photo only, never when the Workdesk opens. No screen before the system prompt (WRITING 9 allows it; it would only add a tap). The proposed NSCameraUsageDescription is below.
  4. Denied: "Camera Off" with Open Settings and Cancel, the same shape as "Microphone Off". Photo Library still works from the menu.
  5. Camera is Apple's own (UIImagePickerController), so Retake and Use Photo are Apple's words and need no upkeep. A Photo Library pick is the confirmation, so it sends at once with no preview.
  6. Reading: the card comes out of the orb as voice cards do (820 ms), with the photo on it. Line "Receipt", second line "Money": the Sidekick is known from the start, so there's no "On It". The big orb shows thinking (the arc).
  7. Added: green core, the Money Sidekick's own line "Corner Market, $42.18, Groceries", "Money" under it, receipt thumbnail on the right. Tapping the card opens Money at that expense.
  8. Can't read the total: red core, "Can't Read Total", button "Add Total". It opens Money's Add Expense with the receipt attached and the merchant, date and category it did read already filled in. Try Again would only read the same photo again.
  9. No connection: grey core, "Receipt", "Waiting for Connection". The photo stays on the phone and goes out by itself, with the existing 1 hour rule (then Not Done with Try Again).
  10. Shell light mode only, like the Workdesk mockup; the shell's dark mode ships later.

NSCameraUsageDescription (proposed):
"Midas uses the camera to photograph receipts and adds them as expenses to your Money Sidekick."

One short active sentence that names the use (WRITING 10, App Review 5.1.1). One string covers both places that ask: jess here, and Take Photo in Money's own Add Receipt menu (the web view uses the app's camera permission).

Open questions (my default in grey)

  1. Should a tap on the photo chip take the photo straight away, with the library one step later (as the iOS Camera's corner thumbnail does)? Default: no, the menu, as briefed.
  2. Words already heard when the chip is tapped: send them, or drop them? Default: send them as their own task.
  3. A photo with nothing readable (blurry, not a receipt): what does the card say? Default: "Can't Read Receipt" with Try Again, which opens the photo menu again. "Can't Read Total" + Add Total stays for when a merchant or items were read but no total.
  4. Tap-through: does Money open over the Workdesk (closing returns to jess) or as a normal Sidekick open? Default: over the Workdesk, at the expense; Close returns to jess.
  5. Long receipts that need two photos. Default: one photo per receipt for now; items past the photo are left out, and the total is what counts.
  6. A non-USD receipt (travel). Default: store the printed number as USD and flag nothing; currency is out of scope while Money is USD only.
  7. Should the home result card (top of Midas) show the thumbnail too? Default: yes, same thumbnail on the right; its words follow the existing card ("Corner Market, $42.18, Groceries" over "Today, 2:14 PM").

Known limits: HTML doesn't show iOS springs, haptics, the real system camera or exact text wrapping. A Midas Test build checks the feel.
